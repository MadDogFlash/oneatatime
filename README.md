# oneatatime (tentative)
an ADHD-friendly hobby tracker that hands you one small thing to do instead of a long list. Tracks which hobbies are going dusty, adapts suggestions to your energy level, and nudges you with the gear you forgot you own. React Native + Expo. 
# Rotation 🎯

**Too many hobbies, one attention span.**

Most to-do apps assume you forgot to do the thing. Rotation assumes you forgot the
thing *exists* — that your soccer ball is under the bed, your practice pad is in a
closet, and picking from a list of ten options is itself the reason you did none of them.

So it doesn't show you a list. It hands you one thing.

## How it works

- **One suggestion at a time.** Weighted toward whichever hobby has gone quietest.
- **Energy-aware tasks.** Low battery gets "find your soccer ball — that's the whole
  task." High energy gets a full session. Both count.
- **Gear-based nudges.** During setup you name where your gear lives, so reminders
  are concrete: *"your practice pad still exists."*
- **Balance view.** See attention split across hobbies this month, and rebalance
  toward the neglected ones — or deliberately lean into one.
- **No streaks, no shame.** Nothing breaks if you skip a week.

## Status

Interactive prototype complete. React Native + Expo build in progress.

## Stack

React Native · Expo · expo-router · expo-notifications (local) · SQLite (on-device)

All data stays on your device. No account, no server.
